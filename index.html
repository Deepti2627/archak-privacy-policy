# Privacy Policy for Archak **Effective Date:** August 11, 2026 Archak is an application designed to help users find and connect with Pandits in and around Ambala. ## 1. Information We Collect From App Users **Archak does not collect personal information from users of the mobile application.** Users can browse and view Pandit information without creating an account or providing personal information to us. We do not collect: * Name * Phone number * Email address * Password * Location * Photos * Contacts * Other personal information from app users ## 2. Pandit Information Displayed in the App The Archak app displays information about Pandits, which may include: * Name * Contact number * Address * Temple name and address * Specialization * Email address * Profile photograph * Other information related to their professional services This information is added, managed, and updated by authorized administrators through the Archak administration system. The purpose of displaying this information is to help users find and contact Pandits. ## 3. How the Information Is Used Pandit information is displayed to provide a directory and connection service. Users can use the information to: * Find Pandits * View Pandit profiles * Learn about their specialization and services * Contact Pandits directly ## 4. Information Sharing Archak does not sell or rent user information. Since the user app does not collect personal information from its users, no user personal information is collected through the app for sale or sharing. Pandit information is intentionally displayed to app users as part of the directory service. ## 5. Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect the information managed through the Archak administration system. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 6. Third-Party Services The Archak application may use third-party technical services for hosting, database storage, and operating the application. These services are used to support the operation of the application. ## 7. Children's Privacy Archak does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The application provides publicly viewable information about Pandits and does not require users to create an account or submit personal information. ## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. ## 9.
